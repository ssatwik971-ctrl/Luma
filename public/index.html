Questions

Short, honest answers.

Is Lumo free?

Lumo itself has no paywall. AI usage is billed by Puter to your own Puter account, so any limits come from there.

Why do I need a Puter account?

Lumo uses Puter.js as its AI layer. Signing in to Puter lets the models run on your credits instead of a shared key.

Where is my data stored?

Chats, memories and settings live in the Lumo database, filtered by your account so only you can read them. You can export or delete them from Settings.

I forgot my password.

Use “Forgot password” on the login page and we will email you a reset link.

Can I upload PDFs or Word files?

Not yet. Images and text or code files are supported today.
